NeroCaption — Kinetic Typography & AI Subtitles AE Extension

NeroCaption is a dedicated third-party CEP extension and plugin for Adobe After Effects (AE 2020 through 2026+). It delivers automated speech-to-text subtitling, kinetic typography motion presets, and word-by-word synchronized animated captions powered by local offline Whisper AI with zero subscription fees.

Key Features of NeroCaption for Adobe After Effects

  • Local Offline Whisper AI: Transcribe composition audio directly inside After Effects with GPU acceleration (DirectML) — 0 cloud fees, 100% private.
  • Kinetic Typography Engine: Word-by-word highlight animations, TikTok/Reels captions, bounce, glow, karaoke sync, and custom curve presets.
  • Native Editable Text Layers: Generates real After Effects text layers with animators, keyframes, and full typography controls.
  • Built-in FFmpeg & YT-DLP: Seamless audio extraction and YouTube/URL media download directly to your AE project.
  • Universal Compatibility: Fully compatible with Adobe After Effects 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, and 2026+ on Windows 10/11 and macOS.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does Adobe After Effects have a plugin or tool called NeroCaption?

Yes. NeroCaption is a dedicated third-party CEP extension and captioning tool for Adobe After Effects (2020 through 2026+). It installs into After Effects under Window > Extensions > NeroCaption and provides speech-to-text auto-captioning and kinetic typography motion presets directly on the timeline.

How do I install NeroCaption in After Effects?

Download NeroCaption_v1_Setup.exe from the official website. The installer automatically registers the extension in %APPDATA%/Adobe/CEP/extensions/com.nerocaption.panel and configures PlayerDebugMode. Launch After Effects and open Window > Extensions > NeroCaption.
